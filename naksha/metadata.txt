about=Naksha (नक्शा, "map") adds a dockable AI chat pane to QGIS. It inspects your project, runs
    Processing algorithms, and verifies its own outputs. Works with local Ollama (free, offline —
    nothing leaves your machine) or any OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic API. GPLv2+.
version=0.4.0
changelog=0.4.0 — answer from a connected AI app without leaving QGIS: pick
    "Connected app" as the source, type in the dock, and the attached MCP client
    reads your message and replies in the dock. New read_chat/send_chat tools.
    The transcript is rebuilt from stacked widgets, so messages are properly
    aligned and the status line updates in place instead of piling up.
    0.3.0 — settings UI (QGIS Options page and standalone dialog), automatic
    detection of whichever AI is available, instant offline commands that need no model
    at all, one-click connection for Claude Desktop/Code, Cursor, Windsurf and VS Code,
    a double-click Claude connector bundle, first-run walkthrough, developer panel, and
    a dependency-free MCP server. 0.2.0 — introspected Processing tool engine,
    plan/verify/self-heal loop, approval modes, threaded turns with cancel, MCP bridge.
    0.1.0 — walking skeleton.
